# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful and
welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Expected Behavior

- Be considerate, constructive, and professional.
- Focus feedback on the work and its impact.
- Respect different viewpoints and experiences.
- Accept correction and adjust behavior when asked.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, publishing private
information, and other conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe or
unwelcoming are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the security contact described in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports will be reviewed and handled confidentially
wherever possible. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or take
other action they consider appropriate.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in project spaces and whenever an individual is
representing the project or its community.
